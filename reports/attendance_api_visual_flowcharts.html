Company and Tenant Database Flow

How each company can have separate data while super admin manages companies centrally.

flowchart TD
    SuperAdmin[Super Admin]:::main --> CreateCompany[Create Company]:::record
    CreateCompany --> Company[Company Record]:::record
    Company --> NeedDb{Separate Database?}:::report
    NeedDb -->|Yes| CreateDb[Create Tenant Database]:::record
    CreateDb --> Migrate[Run Tenant Migrations]:::report
    Migrate --> Sync[Sync Company, Plans, Subscription, Admins]:::record
    NeedDb -->|Already Created| Sync
    Sync --> CompanyAdmin[Company Admin Login]:::main
    CompanyAdmin --> Tenant[Set Current Tenant]:::record
    Tenant --> TenantDb[Read / Write Company Business Data]:::record
    Company --> CentralDb[Central DB Keeps Company + Subscription Control]:::record

Non-Technical Meaning

CompanyThe business/customer using this ERP.
UserEmployee, engineer, or admin who logs in.
SiteWork location used by labour, diesel, stock, and machinery reports.
AttendanceEmployee daily present/absent/leave record.
Labour AttendanceContractor labour daily attendance record.
DPRDaily Progress Report with work details and photos.
Vehicle SheetDaily vehicle usage, KM, time, diesel, and billing.
Stock MovementHistory of material coming in or going out.
